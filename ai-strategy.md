| Approach                                     | Use When                                    | Example Use Cases                                                     | Trade-offs                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Classical Models (Regression, Decision Tree) | Well-defined logic, low cost, deterministic | Fraud detection, demand forecasting, credit scoring                   | + Low computational cost<br>+ Highly interpretable<br>- Limited to learned patterns<br>- Requires structured data                                       |
| Decision Models (JEV, LAYA, CLEF)            | Constrained decisions, audit trails         | Tax determination, eligibility checks, policy compliance              | + Consistent and predictable decisions<br>+ Easy to audit and trace<br>- Requires predefined rules and constraints<br>- Limited flexibility             |
| SLM (Haiku, GPT-5.4 mini/nano)               | Narrow scope, latency-sensitive, edge       | Classification, summarization, FAQ assistants, information extraction | + Lower latency and cost<br>+ Lightweight deployment<br>- Limited complex reasoning<br>- Smaller knowledge capacity                                     |
| LLM (Sonnet, Opus, Astra)                    | High intelligence, dynamic workflows        | Complex document analysis, deep research, coding                      | + Strong reasoning capabilities<br>+ Flexible across use cases<br>- Higher inference cost<br>- Potential hallucinations<br>- Less deterministic outputs |
